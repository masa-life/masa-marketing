About Masa

Masa is a steady, private app for a woman’s whole reproductive story. It starts with cycles, fertility, and pregnancy, and it is built to stay with her as her seasons change. It meets her wherever she is, whether she is mid-IVF, newly paying attention to her body, or not trying for anything at all, and her history follows her when her life changes. Masa shows her numbers plainly, never judges them, and does not assume what her next season should be. There are no ads in any tier, and Masa never sells her data.

Behind Masa

Meet the founder

Bella S.
Founder & CEO, Masa

Bella S. is the founder and CEO of Masa. Her own path to motherhood included natural conception, multiple rounds of IVF, and several pregnancy losses. Through all of it she used one app after another, and none of them could hold what she was living through. With a career in health tech behind her, she knew exactly what was missing, and she built Masa herself. It comes from lived experience, not from guesses about what women want. Bella is also a mother to a toddler.
