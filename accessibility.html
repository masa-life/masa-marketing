Accessibility Statement

Our commitment

Masa is a place women turn to during some of the most demanding seasons of their lives — while exhausted, in pain, grieving, or caring for a newborn. An app that is hard to see, hard to touch, or hard to read aloud fails her at exactly the wrong moment. We treat accessibility as part of the product, not a legal afterthought.

Standards we aim for

We build toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We test public and onboarding pages with automated accessibility audits (axe) and review the app with screen readers and keyboard-only navigation as we ship new surfaces.

What we do

  • Text that resizes and reflows without loss of content, and dark, low-contrast screens kept readable in low light and late-night feeds.
  • Every interactive element reachable and operable by keyboard, with visible focus.
  • Form fields with programmatic labels, so screen readers announce them correctly.
  • Meaningful images carry alternative text; decorative ones are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Color is never the only way information is conveyed — important distinctions also carry text.

Known limitations

Masa is a young product, and we would rather tell you the truth than imply perfection. Some charts and illustrations are not yet fully described for screen readers, and parts of the app have not completed a full manual accessibility review. We keep a list of known gaps and work through it in priority order. If something blocks you, tell us — a report from a real member moves to the front of that list.

Reach us

If you encounter any barrier using Masa or this website — or need content in an alternate format — write to privacy@masa.life. Describe the page or screen and what did not work, and include the device and assistive technology you use if you can. We read every message and respond personally.

Scope

This statement applies to masa.life and the Masa app. Our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy describe the broader commitments that govern Masa.
