Website Privacy Notice

This notice covers masa.life, the website you are on. It does not cover the Masa app, which has its own Privacy Policy.

What this website collects

Nothing you type. masa.life has no forms, no accounts and no sign-in. When you choose to create an account, the button takes you to the Masa app, and the app’s Privacy Policy applies from there.

No tracking

masa.life runs no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or social-media trackers, and sets no cookies of its own. Its fonts and images are served from masa.life itself, so opening a page does not contact any other company.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic browser details when it delivers a page, to send the page to you and to protect the site from abuse. We look only at overall traffic counts, such as how many visits the site had and which pages were viewed, and we do not use them to identify anyone. Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie if it needs to check that a visitor is not automated.

If you email us

If you write to hello@masa.life or another masa.life address, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

Questions

Write to privacy@masa.life.
