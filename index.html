About

Software that listens first

Masa is a calm, private space for people navigating every season of the fertility journey — from trying to conceive and IUI/IVF, through pregnancy, postpartum, and loss. The journey deserves software that never rushes, and holds complexity with dignity.

Masa Life, Inc. is a Delaware corporation building member-centered tools for women's health journeys. Our work is informed by the advocates, and members who have lived these seasons alongside us.

What Masa offers

One place for the whole journey

Track cycles, log how you're doing, prepare for visits, and find carefully curated guidance — without ads, without noise, and without presuming any single outcome.

Journey-aware tracking

Natural TTC, IUI, IVF, pregnancy, postpartum, and rest modes — each with the fields and context that matter for where you are.

Private daily reflection

Journal prompts, check-ins, and a place to note what others might miss — all yours, always accessible.

Clinical visit support

Visit questions, post-visit recaps, and lab-friendly logging so you can show up prepared and leave less confused.

Curated learning

Articles written in a human voice, reviewed for clinical accuracy, and organized by the questions people actually ask.
