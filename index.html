About Masa

Masa is a calm, private space for people navigating every season of the fertility journey — from trying to conceive and IUI/IVF, through pregnancy, postpartum, and loss. We believe the journey deserves software that listens first, never rushes, and holds complexity with dignity.

Masa Life, Inc. is a Delaware corporation building member-centered tools for reproductive health. Our work is informed by the clinicians, advocates, and members who have lived these seasons alongside us.

What Masa offers

A single place to track cycles, log how you're doing, prepare for visits, and find carefully curated guidance — without ads, without noise, and without presuming any single outcome.

Journey-aware tracking

Natural TTC, IUI, IVF, pregnancy, postpartum, and rest modes — each with the fields and context that actually matter for where you are.

Private daily reflection

Journal prompts, check-ins, and a place to note what others might miss — all yours, always accessible.

Clinical visit support

Visit questions, post-visit recaps, and lab-friendly logging so you can show up prepared and leave less confused.

Curated learning

Articles and resources written in a human voice, reviewed for clinical accuracy, and organized by the questions people actually ask.
